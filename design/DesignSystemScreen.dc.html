Spotlight
The target gets a 3px accent ring and a 9px soft halo; the rest of the content dims 38%. The sidebar stays bright.
Completion
The tip disappears the moment the action happens — opening a card, flipping a switch, picking a folder. Skip counts as done.
Keys
foryou · card · knowledge · graph · excluded · sources · hands. Onboarding is its own first-run flow, not a tip.
Storage
Per key, on disk, survives updates. Reset only from Settings → About → Replay.
Voice
Say what happened, not what it is
“Reply to Priya about Goa” — never “Pending action item”.
Every card explains itself
Why this is here · what will happen when you tap · which sources. No card without evidence.
Never sends on its own
The word “Send” is always the user's button. Hands stops one step short and says so.
Brain, reader, Hands
The reader (on this Mac) reads. The brain (your subscription) decides and drafts. Hands acts. Three words, used consistently, never “AI”.
Privacy in plain words
“Erased on sight”, “never left your Mac”, “scrambled bytes and no key”. No “end-to-end”, no acronyms in the UI.
Numbers are honest
Read · kept · not worth keeping · sensitive erased. Always all four; never hide the drops.
Amounts stay out
Summaries omit money, medical and ID specifics. The UI says so where it matters.
Walkthroughs play once
Every screen has one first-time tip, keyed by screen. It completes only when the user does the action it asks for — never on a “Next” button. Completion is stored on disk; only Settings → About → Replay brings it back.
