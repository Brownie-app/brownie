Privacy, in plain words

Brownie reads a lot of your life, so this page is precise about where each kind of data can and cannot go.

Where things go

WhatStays on your MacGoes to the brain you choseGoes to us
Raw files, messages, mail, screenshotsYes — read by the local model, never stored twiceNeverNever
Summaries (short, with private specifics left out)Yes, until the next successful runYes, to build your notes and cardsNever
Your knowledge base (Markdown notes)Yes, in a folder you ownYes, when deciding what needs youNever
Anything flagged sensitive (IDs, cards, passwords, medical records)Nothing — not a title, not a log lineNeverNever
API keys, sign-in tokensYour KeychainOnly to the service they belong toNever
Crash reports, usage counts——Off by default; opt in in Settings

There is no Brownie server

Version 1 has no server of ours at all. Your Mac talks to the model provider you chose, using your own key, and to the services you sign into. We cannot see your data because it never passes through anything we run.

Nothing is sent without your tap

Hands prepares messages, emails and events, and stops before Send, Pay, Submit or Delete. There is no setting that changes this.

Leaving

Settings → About → Uninstall removes the app, the model, the knowledge base, the helper, the login item and your keys. There was never an account to close.

Third parties

Your chosen model provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, or a local server) processes the summaries under its own terms and your own account. Google, Slack, Linear and other sources are read through your own sign-in under their terms.
