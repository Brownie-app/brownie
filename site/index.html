brownie

A house spirit that lives in your Mac and does the chores while you sleep.

Every night, while your Mac is plugged in, Brownie reads what's new in your life — files, messages, notes, mail — with a small model that runs entirely on the machine, and leaves a handful of morning cards: things worth your attention, each ready to do in one tap.

Download for macOS

Reads on your Mac

A 4-billion-parameter model on your own GPU does the reading. Raw files and messages never leave.

Your brain, your key

Deciding what matters and drafting uses a bigger model you already pay for. It only ever sees short, scrubbed summaries.

Hands, not autopilot

Hands can act in your apps — and always stops one step short of Send, Pay or Delete. Those stay yours.

What you get in the morning

"Reply to Priya about Goa — waiting 3 days." "Amma is waiting for the scan report." "Adobe renews Friday." Each card shows why it's there, the evidence, and a draft in your voice. Tap it, and Hands puts everything in place; you press Send.

What we never do

  • Raw data never leaves your Mac. Only summaries reach the model you chose.
  • Sensitive things are erased on sight. ID numbers, cards, passwords, medical records: nothing stored, not even a log line with content.
  • Nothing is sent without your tap. Ever. There is no auto-send setting.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for. There is no Brownie server holding your data.
  • Deletion is total. Uninstall removes the app, the model, the knowledge base, the helper — everything.

What it needs

An Apple-silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later and 8 GB of memory. A 3.7 GB model download, once. Your own API key for ChatGPT/OpenAI or Claude (or a local model server) for the cards; without one, Brownie still keeps your notes.
